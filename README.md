## Roadmap & Ideas

This project is an early prototype and I would love to keep building it out. Here are a few ideas currently on my mind:

- **Static vs. Pop-up Mice:** Keep mice pinned on contributed/active days, while having mice pop out of empty or low-activity cells for the cat to leap onto and paw-tap.
- **Selectable Impact Effects:** Add configurable hit/tap effects such as a shockwave, a popping soap bubble (with tiny droplets), a poof, or a starburst.
- **Customizable Mascots & Palettes:** Make it easy to swap character sets (beyond the GitHub cat silhouette and mice) and customize colors/themes.
- **Configurable Pace & Loop Controls:** Add options for animation speed, loop duration/sampling, and idle-return vs. chain-leaping behavior.

Contributions, ideas, and pull requests are very welcome!
